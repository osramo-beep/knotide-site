Privacy and Data Protection Policy

Effective date: 16/09/2026  ·  Last updated: 16/09/2026

This Privacy and Data Protection Policy ("Policy") explains how Knotide Bio, Lda. ("Knotide Bio", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, discloses and protects personal data, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR") and Portuguese Law No. 58/2019. It applies to personal data we hold or control, including data processed on our behalf by third-party service providers.

1. Who we are

The data controller is Knotide Bio, Lda., a company registered in Portugal under NIPC 519634713, with registered office at Avenida da República, n.º 1333, 1.º traseiras, 4430-204 Vila Nova de Gaia, Portugal.

2. What personal data we collect

We collect personal data that individuals ("you") provide directly, for example when you contact us through our website, propose a collaboration, apply for a role, or engage with us as a partner or service provider. Depending on the nature of your interaction with us, this may include your name, email address, telephone number, organisation, job title, and the content of your message.

3. How and why we use your data

We use personal data only for purposes that are compatible with why it was collected, relying on the following legal bases under Article 6 GDPR:

  • Consent, where you have explicitly agreed, for example to receive information about our activities.
  • Performance of a contract, to manage a collaboration, service, or partnership with you or your organisation.
  • Legitimate interests, to respond to enquiries, maintain the security of our systems, and manage our relationship with researchers, partners and prospective collaborators, provided these interests do not override your rights and freedoms.
  • Legal obligation, where processing is required to comply with applicable law, including accounting, tax and company law obligations.

We do not use your personal data for a new purpose incompatible with the one notified to you without first informing you and, where required, obtaining your consent.

4. Disclosure of personal data

We may share personal data with:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf (for example, hosting, accounting or IT support providers), under written agreements that require them to protect the data in line with this Policy.
  • Public authorities, where disclosure is required by law or to protect our legal rights.
  • Funding bodies or consortium partners, strictly to the extent necessary to manage a joint project or application you are involved in.

5. International transfers

We do not generally transfer personal data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where a transfer is necessary, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with Chapter V of the GDPR, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

6. Retention of personal data

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable legal, accounting or reporting obligations. When personal data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

7. Security of personal data

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration, including access controls, secure storage and staff awareness of data protection obligations. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and while we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Request erasure of your data, where applicable;
  • Request restriction of, or object to, certain processing;
  • Request portability of data you provided to us;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), at www.cnpd.pt.

We aim to respond to any request within one month of receipt. Where a request is complex or we have received a high number of requests, this period may be extended by a further two months, in which case we will inform you of the extension and the reasons for it.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in applicable law. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Continued use of our website or services after an update constitutes acknowledgement of the revised Policy.
