Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Knotide Bio, Lda. ("Knotide Bio", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why, and what rights you have, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Portuguese Law no. 58/2019.

1. Who we are

Knotide Bio, Lda. · NIPC 519634713
Avenida da República, n.º 1333, 1.º traseiras, 4430-204 Vila Nova de Gaia, Portugal
Contact: info@knotidebio.pt

Knotide Bio is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

2. What data we collect

  • Contact form: name, email address, organisation (optional), and the content of your message, when you choose to submit them.
  • Website usage: aggregated, non-identifying statistics such as pages viewed, country-level location, device type and referring site. We use privacy-focused analytics that does not use cookies, does not use any device identifiers, and cannot be used to identify an individual visitor.

3. Why we process this data

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form, on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating this website and communicating with people who contact us, and, where relevant, to take steps prior to entering into a business relationship.
  • To understand overall website usage and improve the site, on the basis of our legitimate interest. This data is aggregated and does not identify you.

4. Who has access to your data

Contact-form submissions are processed on our behalf by Formspree, Inc., which acts as our data processor and delivers submissions to our email. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. International data transfers

Formspree is based in the United States. Where your data is processed there, it is subject to the safeguards described in Formspree's own privacy policy, available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep contact-form submissions for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records, and in any case no longer than 24 months, unless a longer period is required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and Portuguese Law no. 58/2019, you have the right to access, correct, or request erasure of your personal data, to object to or restrict our processing of it, and to request its portability. You may also lodge a complaint with the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), the Portuguese supervisory authority, at www.cnpd.pt.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@knotidebio.pt.

8. Cookies

This website does not use cookies.

9. Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date of the most recent update is shown at the top of this page.
